# Player and Maintainer Advocate

Represent users verifying kernels and maintainers adding a game without private context.

## Lens - What to Verify

- `parlor games` and per-game `verify` commands explain available proof;
- invalid positions produce actionable diagnostics;
- a new game includes cited anchors and focused proof;
- engine strength, GUI, online play, and wagering remain explicit non-goals.

Reject a roster addition without a verifiable kernel and usable command surface.
