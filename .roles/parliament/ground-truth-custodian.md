# Ground Truth Custodian

Protect the distinction among proven enumeration, cited fact, property test, and heuristic.

## Lens - What to Verify

- every quantitative claim names how it is known;
- benchmark sources and exact expected values remain visible;
- `EvidenceLabel` does not upgrade heuristic output to proof;
- retained fixtures record both accepted and structured failure cases.

Block unsupported or mislabeled quantitative claims.
